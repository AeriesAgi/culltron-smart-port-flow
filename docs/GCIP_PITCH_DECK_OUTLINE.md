# GCIP South Africa — Pitch Deck Outline
### Culltron Smart Port Flow (12 slides)

> Use with `docs/GCIP_SOUTH_AFRICA_SUBMISSION.md` (narrative) and `docs/GCIP_IMPACT_AND_SDG.md`
> (impact maths). Keep every claim honest: **synthetic-data prototype, modelled impact, no live
> partnerships.** `[APPLICANT TO COMPLETE]` markers need verified content before pitching.

---

**Slide 1 — Title & hook**
- Culltron Smart Port Flow · "AI that turns port congestion into one clean, approved action."
- Cleantech category: Energy Efficiency / Clean Transport. Tagline, logo, presenter name.
- Footer: *Prototype on synthetic demo data.*

**Slide 2 — The problem (make it visceral)**
- SA ports are among the world's most congested; thousands of diesel trucks idle in queues daily.
- Idling truck ≈ **8 kg CO₂/idling-hour** + NOₓ/PM into port-adjacent communities + wasted diesel +
  demurrage. Load-shedding makes it worse.
- Root cause: **coordination gap** — control room, fleet owner and driver never share one picture.

**Slide 3 — The insight**
- Congestion is a *disconnect*, not just a capacity problem. Fix the coordination and you cut idling,
  emissions and cost at the same time — with existing assets.

**Slide 4 — The solution**
- Sense → **Reason** → Approve → Execute loop across three surfaces (control room, fleet, driver).
- Cross-domain engine links gate + berth + yard + emissions + disruption into **one prioritised,
  quantified, human-approved action**.

**Slide 5 — Product demo (live or screenshots)**
- The reasoning headline: *"Berth pressure + gate backlog = N trucks idling avg M min → X CO₂,
  ~RY demurrage risk → re-sequence Z trucks, expedite gate. [Approve / Modify / Reject]."*
- Driver companion check-in → fleet tracker update → immutable audit entry.

**Slide 6 — Why it's credible cleantech**
- Deterministic, transparent CO₂/diesel maths — **works with no AI key**; reproducible & auditable.
- Impact quantified and logged at every decision = **built-in MRV** for GEF/UNIDO.
- Human-approval gates; non-invasive (coordinates existing trucks/gates/berths).

**Slide 7 — Environmental impact (modelled, honest)**
- Unit: **8.04 kg CO₂ avoided per truck-idling-hour eliminated**.
- Pilot (~25 trucks): **~13–25 tCO₂e/yr** + ~R165k diesel saved.
- Terminal scale: **~0.8–1.2 ktCO₂e/yr** + ~R9m diesel saved.
- Label clearly: *modelled potential, verified in pilot.* SDGs: 13, 9, 11, 7, 8, 5.

**Slide 8 — Market & business model**
- SA ports + road-freight fleets (KZN first) → other African ports.
- SaaS: **per berth / per fleet vehicle / per port**; Demo → Pilot → Enterprise tiers.
- `[APPLICANT: TAM/SAM/SOM]`.

**Slide 9 — Traction & readiness**
- Working prototype, **TRL 5–6**, full loop on synthetic data; connector-ready architecture;
  documented pilot-to-production path. Be explicit: **no live customer yet** — pilot is the next step.

**Slide 10 — Team & gender inclusion**
- `[APPLICANT: founders, domain experience, advisors]` + concrete gender-mainstreaming plan.

**Slide 11 — Roadmap & the ask**
- Now → GCIP acceleration → **supervised pilot (measured reductions)** → productisation → multi-port.
- Ask: mentoring, market access, investor readiness, **grant/seed** — `[APPLICANT: amount + use]`.

**Slide 12 — Vision & close**
- "Cleaner, faster, fairer ports — starting in South Africa, scaling across Africa."
- Contact + demo link/credentials. Footer: *synthetic-data prototype; impact figures are modelled.*

### Delivery notes
- Lead with the **idling = CO₂** equation; it makes the cleantech case in one line.
- Show the **live Approve/Reject gate and the audit trail** — judges reward governance + MRV.
- Never overclaim: say "modelled" and "pilot will measure," not "we save X tonnes."
